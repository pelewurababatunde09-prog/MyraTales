Privacy policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This page explains the limited information used by the current MyraTales front-end. It is a general template and should be reviewed and completed by the site owner before public or commercial launch.

Information stored in your browser

When you change the color theme, MyraTales stores your preference in your browser's local storage under the key theme. This is a functional preference, not an account, and it is not sent to MyraTales by the current front-end.

Third-party services

The page currently requests fonts from Google Fonts and collection images from Unsplash. Those requests may expose technical information such as your IP address, browser, and the page requested to the relevant provider. Review each provider's current privacy terms and replace these assets with self-hosted files if reducing third-party requests is important.

Cookies and analytics

The current files do not intentionally set cookies or include analytics, advertising pixels, social widgets, payment tools, or embedded media players. If any of these are added later, this policy and any required consent mechanism must be updated before use.

Children and sensitive information

Do not submit sensitive personal information through this website. The current front-end does not provide user accounts or a submission form.

Your rights

Privacy rights depend on where you live. The site owner should add a real contact method and jurisdiction-specific instructions for access, correction, deletion, objection, portability, and complaints where applicable.

Local compliance review

This policy cannot determine the law that applies without the operator's location, business model, audience, hosting setup, and third-party contracts. Obtain local legal or privacy advice before launch, especially if the site serves children, collects submissions, uses analytics, or operates in a regulated jurisdiction.
